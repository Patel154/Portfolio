The generic Small Projects of WT card was removed in favor of its substantial SkillSwap subproject. Project-specific details in recognition copy were removed where they referenced unmatched projects; existing recognition and employment history were otherwise left in place.

The redundant research section was consolidated into Selected Projects after removing Brain Tumor Classification and moving RetinaBridge. Project filters, terminal output, introductory copy, and skill groups now describe the verified set. Decorative skill rings no longer encode self-assessed proficiency percentages.
